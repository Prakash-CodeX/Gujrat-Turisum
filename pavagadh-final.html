01 — THE PLACE

MORE THAN A DESTINATION

Where the earth
meets belief.

Pavagadh rises from the plains of Panchmahal with a landscape shaped by nature, faith and centuries of history. At its summit, the revered Kalika Mata Temple draws pilgrims; below, the Champaner region preserves remarkable traces of the past.

BEFORE YOU GO

04 — MAKE IT A GOOD JOURNEY

A few things
to know.

01

Check access first

Confirm temple access, ropeway operating status and timings through current local sources before travelling.

02

Travel comfortably

Carry drinking water, sun protection and comfortable footwear suitable for steps and uneven paths.

03

Plan around crowds

Festival days and peak periods can be busy. Allow extra time and follow on-site directions.
