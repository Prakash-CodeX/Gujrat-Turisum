THE SPIRIT OF DWARKA
GET TO KNOW DWARKA

Tradition with a coastal soul.

Dwarka is a historic coastal city in Gujarat, widely associated with Lord Krishna. The revered Dwarkadhish Temple is a major landmark and draws pilgrims and visitors throughout the year.

Beyond its spiritual importance, Dwarka offers coastal views, traditional markets, local cuisine and opportunities to explore the cultural heritage of western Gujarat.

Destination Facts

  • Location Devbhoomi Dwarka, Gujarat
  • Known for Dwarkadhish Temple
  • Landscape Arabian Sea coastline
  • Experience Heritage and pilgrimage
