A SYMBOL THAT STANDS TALL

More than a monument.
A story of unity.

Standing 182 metres tall, the Statue of Unity honours Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and his contribution to uniting India.

Make a day of the destination: take in the scale of the monument, explore the surrounding attractions and pause to enjoy the striking scenery around the Narmada River.
