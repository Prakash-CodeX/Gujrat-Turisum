THE KING OF GIR
GET TO KNOW GIR

Nature in its purest form.

Gir National Park is located in Gujarat, India. It is famous for protecting the Asiatic lion and is one of India's important wildlife destinations.

The forest includes dry deciduous trees, grasslands, rivers and a variety of plants and animals. It is a popular destination for nature lovers and wildlife photographers.

Important Facts

  • Location Gujarat, India
  • Famous for Asiatic lions
  • Main activity Wildlife safari
  • Natural features Forests and grasslands
